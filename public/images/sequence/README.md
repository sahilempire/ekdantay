# Scroll sequence images

Drop the generated images here, named exactly as below. Prompts are in
docs/image-prompts.md.

  01-skull.png        skull, three-quarter view
  02-jaw.png          lower jaw arch
  03-tooth-in-bone.png single molar seated in bone
  04-exploded.png     exploded tooth  <- gets cut into 5 layers
  05-whole.png        whole tooth, same camera as 04
  06-whitening.png    half-stained tooth
  07-braces.png       arch with brackets
  08-implant.png      implant in bone
  09-cracked.png      cracked tooth

PNG or JPG is fine; they get keyed to transparency and converted to WebP.

The only two things that must be right in the source: a PURE BLACK
background (a glow or gradient bleeds grey fringing when keyed out), and
in 04, parts that do not touch each other (touching parts cannot be cut
apart reliably).
